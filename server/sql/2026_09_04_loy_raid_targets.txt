What raid_target does on Ascendant:
  - Encounter scaling uses the 'raid' profile (largest resist delta)
  - Level-block raid bonus loot (1-3 items from loy_raid_pools) instead of the rare_spawn roll
  - Depopped in Normal-mode expeditions; present in Raid-mode expeditions
  - Ascendant caster rules: PureCasterRaidTargetDmgBonus / DoTCasterRaidTargetDmgBonus apply
  - Kill logged as KILLED_RAID_NPC player event
